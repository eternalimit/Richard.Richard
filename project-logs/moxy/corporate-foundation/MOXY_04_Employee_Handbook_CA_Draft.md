# MOXY Industries
## Employee Handbook - California Draft

DRAFT FOR COUNSEL / ADOPTION

Effective date: [TO BE ADOPTED] | Version 0.1

This handbook is a policy framework, not an employment contract. California employment counsel and payroll/HR specialists should review it before employee distribution. Local ordinances and employee-specific agreements may require additional or different terms.

### 1. Welcome and Operating Principles
MOXY exists to build safe, useful and manufacturable products for customers. Employees are expected to work with integrity, protect people, report problems early, preserve evidence, and improve the system rather than hide failures.

Safety before schedule. Quality before shipment. Evidence before closure. Respect for every employee. Accurate labor, material and financial records. No retaliation for good-faith reporting.

### 2. Employment Relationship
Employment classifications, exempt/nonexempt status, full-time/part-time status, compensation, work location and reporting relationships are documented in the Employee Master and offer documentation. Nothing in this handbook changes rights that cannot lawfully be waived. [California at-will language, if used, must be reviewed and approved by counsel and included consistently in offer materials.]

### 3. Equal Employment Opportunity, Harassment and Retaliation
MOXY prohibits unlawful discrimination, harassment and retaliation. Employees may report concerns to their supervisor, OLM/HR, the GM, or another designated reporting channel. Employees are not required to report a concern to a person involved in the concern. Complaints will be addressed promptly and as confidentially as reasonably possible.

### 4. Reasonable Accommodation
MOXY will engage in a timely, good-faith interactive process and provide reasonable accommodation as required by applicable law for disability, pregnancy-related needs, religious practices and other protected circumstances. Employees should contact OLM/HR to request accommodation; no special wording is required.

### 5. Employee Registration and Position Control
Before performing work, each employee must be entered into the Office of Labor Management Employee Master with a permanent Employee ID, assigned Position ID, department, supervisor, cost center, work center/shift where applicable, employment classification and required training. Employee IDs are never reassigned.

### 6. Timekeeping and Labor Punch Clock
Nonexempt employees must accurately record all time worked using the approved system. Off-the-clock work is prohibited. Employees must record required attendance punches and job-labor transactions as instructed. Employees must report missed or incorrect punches promptly. No employee or supervisor may falsify, erase or silently rewrite time records. Corrections preserve the original punch, reason, requestor, approver, timestamp and audit history.

Paid hours reconcile to direct labor, indirect labor, meetings, training, approved downtime and other paid time. An unexplained variance is an exception for review, not automatic permission to withhold earned wages.

### 7. Work Schedule, Meals, Rest Periods and Overtime
Schedules are assigned by management and may change based on operations and applicable notice requirements. Nonexempt employees must follow California meal and rest-period requirements and must immediately report any missed, late, short, interrupted or otherwise noncompliant period. Employees must obtain approval before overtime when required by policy, but all hours actually worked must still be reported and paid in accordance with law.

### 8. Pay Practices
MOXY will provide required wage notices, wage statements and timely payroll. Payroll deductions will be made only as permitted by law. Employees should promptly report payroll questions or suspected errors to OLM/HR or Finance. Good-faith wage concerns may be raised without retaliation.

### 9. Attendance and Leave
Employees should report absences and lateness using the designated process. MOXY will administer paid sick leave and other protected leaves required by California, federal and applicable local law. Leave rights may depend on employer size, tenure, hours worked, location and qualifying reason; OLM/HR will provide the applicable notice and process when a leave need arises.

### 10. Lactation Accommodation
MOXY will provide lactation accommodation and an appropriate location as required by California law. Employees may request accommodation through OLM/HR or any manager. Retaliation for requesting or using lactation accommodation is prohibited.

### 11. Safety, Product Testing and Facility Conduct
Employees must follow safety rules, training, PPE requirements, equipment lockout/energy-control procedures where applicable, battery-handling rules, flight-test boundaries and emergency procedures. Only trained and authorized employees may operate controlled equipment or enter restricted test areas. Unsafe conditions must be reported immediately and may be placed on HOLD.

### 12. Quality, Traceability and TCGE Reporting
Employees must maintain accurate records for materials, work orders, Unit IDs, firmware, calibration, inspection, test, scrap, rework and shipment. No issue moves between DMS tiers without a TCGE handoff report unless an emergency or protected-reporting route requires immediate escalation. Missing independent validation remains HOLD; employees must not manufacture evidence or change records to create a PASS.

### 13. Information Security and Confidentiality
Employees must protect confidential product designs, source code, customer information, employee information, supplier terms, financial data and credentials. Access is limited to legitimate business need. Company systems may be monitored and audited to the extent permitted by law. Confidentiality rules do not prohibit legally protected discussion of wages, hours or working conditions or lawful reporting to government agencies.

### 14. Conflicts, Gifts and Business Conduct
Employees must avoid undisclosed conflicts of interest, bribery, kickbacks, falsified records, theft and misuse of company property. Potential conflicts should be disclosed to management or OLM/HR. Supplier and customer decisions must serve MOXY's legitimate business interests.

### 15. Workplace Conduct and Violence Prevention
Threats, intimidation and workplace violence are prohibited. Employees should report immediate danger to emergency services and facility leadership. MOXY will maintain workplace violence prevention measures and training as applicable to its operations and California requirements.

### 16. Performance, Corrective Action and Separation
Managers are expected to set clear expectations, coach performance and document material issues. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, MOXY may use coaching, warnings, reassignment, suspension or separation. The company may skip steps when warranted. Final pay, benefits notices, property return and system access termination will be handled under applicable requirements.

### 17. Required Notices and Updates
OLM/HR maintains required workplace postings and employee notices. Policies will be updated when laws, operations or benefit programs change. California now requires an annual Workplace Know Your Rights notice; OLM/HR will distribute the current required notice using an approved method and language as applicable.

### 18. Reporting Channels
Employees may report concerns through supervisor, OLM/HR, GM, safety/quality channels, or another lawful protected reporting route as appropriate.

### 19. Employee Acknowledgment

I understand that I am responsible for reading and following applicable policies and that policies may be changed subject to applicable law. I understand that this handbook is not a guarantee of employment for any specific duration and does not replace rights provided by law or a signed agreement.
