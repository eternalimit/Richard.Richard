# MOXY Industries
## Organization Charter and Operating Structure

DRAFT FOR COUNSEL / ADOPTION

### 1. Corporate Purpose
MOXY Industries is organized to design, validate, manufacture, market, sell, service, and continuously improve safe technology-enabled character products and related systems. The company operates through evidence-based product development, disciplined manufacturing, customer feedback, and auditable financial and labor controls.

### 2. Governance Hierarchy
Shareholders -> Board of Directors -> Chief Executive / General Manager -> Functional Leaders -> Tier Groups -> Employees.

The Board governs the corporation. Management executes the approved strategy. Daily Management System (DMS) tiers control day-to-day operating performance. TCGE handoffs govern escalation between tier points.

### 3. Initial Organization
Wave 1 organization: 18 positions across Executive, Product, Mechanical, Electrical, Software/Controls, Manufacturing, Quality/Test, Supply Chain, Operations, Finance and Sales/Customer Development.

### 4. Office of Labor Management (OLM)
OLM maintains the Employee Master, Position Master, timekeeping controls, training and qualification records, supervisor approvals, labor reporting, and payroll-ready time data. Employee IDs are permanent and never reused. Position IDs belong to the organization and may be refilled.

### 5. DMS and TCGE Control
Daily operating sequence: DATA -> T1 -> T2 -> T3 -> EXECUTE -> CHECK -> CLOSE. No issue is promoted from one tier to another without a TCGE handoff record identifying Reality, Inference, independent Echo, Knowledge state, impact, action, owner, due date, evidence and receiving tier. Missing validation remains HOLD.

### 6. SAP / OLAP Management Structure
The management system is designed around Company Code, Controlling Area, Profit Center, Cost Center, Plant, Storage Location, Work Center, Material, Production Order, Employee, Customer, General Ledger and Time. Shared dimensions connect Production, Personnel, Finance and Sales cubes.

### 7. Adoption
This charter becomes effective only when adopted by the authorized corporate body. It is an internal operating document and does not itself form a legal entity.
