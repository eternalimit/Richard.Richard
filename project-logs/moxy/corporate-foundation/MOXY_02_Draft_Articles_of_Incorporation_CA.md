### Filing Checklist
- Confirm final entity name and California name availability.
- Confirm corporation vs. LLC choice with legal/tax advisers.
- Confirm authorized shares and founder/investor capitalization.
- Identify agent for service of process and addresses.
- File Articles through California bizfile Online.
- File the initial Statement of Information within the applicable deadline.
- Adopt bylaws, appoint directors/officers, approve initial resolutions, issue shares only with proper approvals and securities/tax advice.
- Obtain EIN and complete federal/state employer registrations before payroll.

### Drafting Note
California Secretary of State guidance states that Articles of Incorporation form a California corporation and that bylaws and meeting minutes are maintained internally rather than filed with the Secretary of State. This draft intentionally leaves fact-dependent filing fields unresolved rather than inventing them.
