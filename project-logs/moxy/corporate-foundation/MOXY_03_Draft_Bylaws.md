# MOXY Industries, Inc.
## Draft Corporate Bylaws

DRAFT FOR COUNSEL / ADOPTION

These bylaws are a governance draft for review and formal adoption after the corporation is formed. They should be reviewed by California corporate counsel, especially before issuing equity, raising capital, adopting indemnification arrangements, or changing board/shareholder rights.

### ARTICLE I - OFFICES
The principal executive office shall be fixed by the Board of Directors. The corporation may maintain other offices as the Board or officers determine. Corporate records shall be maintained as required by applicable law.

### ARTICLE II - SHAREHOLDERS
Annual meetings shall be held at a date and time fixed by the Board for the election of directors and other proper business. Special meetings may be called by persons authorized under applicable law, the Articles, or these bylaws. Notice, quorum, voting, proxies, written consents, remote participation and record dates shall be governed by applicable California law and any valid Board procedures.

### ARTICLE III - BOARD OF DIRECTORS
The business and affairs of the corporation shall be managed under the direction of the Board. Initial authorized number of directors: [1-3, TO BE FIXED IN ORGANIZATIONAL ACTION]. Any change shall comply with the Articles, these bylaws and California law. The Board may establish committees and delegate authority to the extent permitted by law. Directors shall disclose conflicts and comply with approved conflict-of-interest procedures.

### ARTICLE IV - OFFICERS
The corporation shall have the officers required by California law and such additional officers as the Board appoints. The Board shall define authority, compensation and succession. One person may hold multiple offices to the extent permitted by law. The General Manager / COO may lead daily operations subject to Board-approved authority and reserved matters.

### ARTICLE V - AUTHORITY AND RESERVED MATTERS
Board approval is required for matters reserved by law and for material actions designated by Board policy, including major capital commitments, equity issuance, material borrowing, acquisitions, disposition of substantial assets and changes to the corporation's strategic direction. Management shall operate within approved budgets, delegated authority matrices and internal controls.

### ARTICLE VI - SHARES AND RECORDS
Shares may be issued only pursuant to Board authorization and applicable securities, tax and corporate requirements. The corporation shall maintain an accurate capitalization table, stock ledger and supporting approvals. No employee code, Unit ID, token, internal ledger entry or TCGE record constitutes corporate equity unless formally authorized and issued.

### ARTICLE VII - INDEMNIFICATION
The corporation shall indemnify directors, officers, employees and agents to the fullest extent permitted by California law, subject to applicable standards, exclusions, procedures and Board-approved insurance arrangements.

### ARTICLE VIII - FINANCIAL AND INTERNAL CONTROLS
The fiscal year shall be fixed by the Board. Finance shall maintain books and records, budgets, forecasts, approval controls and financial reporting. SAP-style organizational master data and OLAP reporting may be used as management systems but do not replace statutory books, tax records or legally required corporate records.

### ARTICLE IX - DMS / TCGE OPERATING GOVERNANCE
The Daily Management System shall use Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 operating reviews. Cross-tier operational handoffs shall use the approved TCGE report. TCGE is an internal evidence-control framework and does not alter statutory duties of directors, officers, managers or employees. Safety, legal, ethics or harassment concerns may bypass normal tier escalation when immediate reporting or protected reporting is appropriate.

### ARTICLE X - AMENDMENTS
These bylaws may be amended, repealed or replaced by the Board or shareholders to the extent permitted by the Articles and California law. Amendments shall be recorded in the corporation's official records.
